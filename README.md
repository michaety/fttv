## Features

- **Remote control:** browse the whole site with the arrows and Select.
- **Search:** MDE's search box is in the header.
- **Scrubbing:** left and right show a preview of where you'll jump to.
- **63% less CPU:** compared with v1.0, measured on MDE's homepage.
- **Subtitles:** on MDE, from the Menu (☰) button.
- **Autoplay:** the next episode starts when one finishes. Back cancels.
- **Auto-updates:** the app offers new versions when it opens.

## Requirements

- A Fire TV device. These were tested on a Fire TV Stick 4K (2nd gen).
- An account on the site, **with a password**. Google sign-in doesn't work
  in the app, so if you only use Google, set a password with "forgot
  password" on the site first.

## Installing

1. On the Fire TV, open Settings → My Fire TV → About, and select your
   device name 7 times to turn on Developer Options.
2. In Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → Install Unknown Apps, turn
   it on for
   [Downloader](https://www.amazon.com.au/AFTVnews-com-Downloader/dp/B01N0BP507).
3. In Downloader, enter one of these addresses:
   - `https://github.com/michaety/fttv/releases/latest/download/fishtank-tv.apk`
   - `https://github.com/michaety/fttv/releases/latest/download/mde-tv.apk`
4. Open the app and sign in.

When an update installs, move right to **INSTALL** on Fire TV's confirm
screen, because it starts on Cancel.

## Notes

These are the websites in a TV-friendly wrapper, so expect some rough edges,
and a site redesign may break things until the app is updated. They don't
collect anything or bypass anything. I made them for myself, and I'll only
